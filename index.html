PROTECTING PEOPLE & THE BRAND

Security, rules & nationwide branches

Clear rules, careful review and restricted staff access are part of the plan for a safer marketplace.

🛡️

Fraud prevention

Report suspicious listings, review posts before publication, limit repeated submissions, keep an investigation record and remove content that breaks marketplace rules.

🔐

Account & data security

Production launch must use verified sign-in, server-enforced permissions, least-privilege access, secure file uploads, rate limits, backups and audit logs. Never share OTPs, passwords or secret keys.

🏢

State branches

NaijaMarket can be organised into state offices and branch teams. Each branch should manage only its assigned area; the overall owner retains national oversight and can review branch activity.

⚖️

Fair enforcement

Reports trigger investigation, not automatic guilt. Staff decisions should be recorded, sensitive actions reviewed, and affected users given a way to appeal where appropriate.

Important: These website pages explain the intended rules. Real security controls, branch permissions and legal documents are not active until the Supabase backend, policies, staff accounts and final legal review are implemented and tested.

HELP & ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about buying, selling, safety, fees and how NaijaMarket is being prepared for launch.

What is NaijaMarket?

NaijaMarket is being developed as a Nigeria-wide marketplace where people and businesses can discover goods, property, vehicles and business opportunities. The current website is a preview; some features are not connected to a live service yet.

Can I browse listings for free?

Yes. Browsing the preview does not require payment. Some listings shown now are sample demonstrations, not verified offers. Real approved listings should replace these examples before public launch.

How do I post an item for sale?

Use a “Post an item” button and follow the form. In this preview, submissions are not yet connected to a secure live database, so do not rely on the form to publish or store a real listing. Live posting will be enabled after the backend is connected and tested.

How are listings approved?

The intended process is for new listings to remain pending until an authorised moderator reviews them. Approval checks can reduce risk, but they cannot guarantee that every seller, item or claim is genuine.

Does NaijaMarket guarantee sellers, goods or property?

No. A review is not a guarantee. Before paying, inspect the item in person where possible, verify the seller’s identity and ownership documents, and independently confirm property titles or vehicle documents with the appropriate professionals or authorities.

How can I report a scam or suspicious listing?

Use the “Report a suspicious listing” or “Report a concern” option when available. Provide the listing details and a clear explanation, but do not post your password, PIN, OTP, bank details or other private information. Reporting and investigation tools in this preview still need a live backend and support workflow.

Does NaijaMarket collect payment or hold money for buyers?

Not in this preview. Do not send money to anyone claiming that NaijaMarket has guaranteed a deal, escrowed funds or processed a payment unless an official, verified payment service is clearly launched. Any future charges and commissions must be shown before you agree to pay.

Are there listing fees or commissions?

Browsing and basic listing submission are intended to be free at launch, but final terms have not been confirmed. Paid promotions or service commissions may be introduced later only with clear prices, terms and a working payment system.

Can NaijaMarket convert airtime to cash?

Not yet. The airtime-to-cash section is currently an information/enquiry concept, not a working conversion or payment service. Do not send airtime, money, PINs or OTPs to anyone based on the preview.

Can NaijaMarket track a lost phone or a vehicle?

The website does not directly track devices. For a phone, use the official device-finding service for your own account. Vehicle tracking normally requires a properly installed GPS tracker and an authorised provider account. Never track a person or device without lawful authority or consent.

Will NaijaMarket have offices and admins in different states?

That is the planned expansion model. State branch managers and staff should have individual accounts with access limited to their assigned duties and branch. The overall owner should retain national oversight. These roles are not secure or operational until server-side permissions and staff accounts are configured and tested.

How will NaijaMarket protect my information?

The production version should use secure sign-in, limited staff permissions, protected database rules, safe file uploads, backups and audit logs. These controls must be implemented and tested before real personal information is collected. Never share your password, banking PIN or OTP with anyone claiming to be support.

What if a listing is rejected or my account is restricted?

The intended process is to review the reason, record important moderation decisions and provide an appropriate way to ask for a review. A report should trigger investigation, not automatically prove that a person has done something wrong.

Does the disclaimer remove all responsibility from NaijaMarket?

No. The disclaimer explains important limitations and user responsibilities, but it does not remove every legal obligation or replace Nigerian law. Final Terms of Service, privacy information and dispute procedures should be reviewed by a qualified Nigerian lawyer before launch.

What is the difference between a customer account and an agent account?

Customers use their account to browse and manage their own activity. Agents are separate applicants who may help with approved marketplace tasks in an assigned state or area only after the overall manager verifies and approves them. An application does not automatically grant agent access.

Can anyone register as an agent in any Nigerian state?

People may submit an agent application and select their state or FCT. Applications must be reviewed before approval. NaijaMarket may request lawful identity or reference checks, and must explain what information is collected and why. Do not pay an unofficial person to obtain an agent account.

Who controls the whole NaijaMarket platform?

The intended structure has one overall owner/manager with national oversight. Agents do not receive owner privileges. The real hierarchy must be enforced in the backend; a public webpage or a role selected on a signup form cannot securely establish manager authority.

How do agent earnings and withdrawals work?

The agent earnings room is currently a design preview only. No real balance, commission, deposit or withdrawal is active. Before launch, NaijaMarket must publish clear commission rules, record earnings in a protected server-side ledger, verify payments and provide a transparent withdrawal process. Never pay anyone to unlock a displayed demo balance.

Can you guarantee that no one will ever hack an account?

No website can honestly guarantee that it can never be hacked. NaijaMarket should reduce risk through strong unique passwords, multi-factor authentication for the manager and staff, secure recovery, server-enforced permissions, database row-level security, rate limiting, monitoring, backups and regular security testing. These controls must be implemented and verified before launch.

How do I contact NaijaMarket support?

Official support contacts will be displayed here once they have been configured and verified. Until then, be cautious of unofficial accounts claiming to represent NaijaMarket, and never share passwords, OTPs or banking credentials.

Please note: NaijaMarket is still being prepared for launch. These answers describe intended policies and planned features; they do not mean every service, security control or support process is already active.
